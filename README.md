# Growify Digital — Marketing Performance & Budget Optimization

**Associate Data Analyst Assessment | Analyze. Diagnose. Reallocate. Grow.**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes digital marketing performance for Growify Digital to identify inefficient ad spend, uncover high-performing campaigns, and develop a data-driven budget reallocation strategy.

The objective is to achieve **25% sales growth without increasing the existing marketing budget** by reallocating spending toward higher-performing campaigns, improving creative effectiveness, and optimizing conversion efficiency.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business generates ₹14.30M in Shopify sales with a paid-media spend of ₹6.28M. However, marketing expenditure is heavily concentrated on Facebook, which delivers substantially lower return on ad spend (ROAS) than Google.

The key business questions are:

- Which marketing channels and campaigns deliver the best returns?
- Which campaigns and creatives are underperforming?
- How should the existing marketing budget be reallocated?
- Is achieving 25% sales growth possible without additional marketing expenditure?

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Channel Performance

| Metric | Google | Facebook |
|---|---:|---:|
| Approximate ad spend | ₹397.6K | ₹5.88M |
| Return on ad spend (ROAS) | 5.86x | 1.89x |

Google demonstrates significantly stronger advertising efficiency, while Facebook accounts for approximately 93.7% of paid-media expenditure.

### 2. Campaign Performance

**Top-performing campaigns**

- **Google LuxuryP Search India 12/12/25:** 12.08x ROAS
- **Google P.max HP EOSS India:** 6.40x ROAS
- **Facebook MOF & BOF India + International:** 2.28x ROAS, with 279 purchases

**Underperforming campaigns and creatives**

- **Facebook INT Direct:** 1.11x ROAS on approximately ₹423K spend.
- Several Flat 40% and Special Prices creatives deliver less than 0.7x ROAS.
- Multiple individual advertisements generate less than 1x ROAS.

These findings indicate opportunities to reduce inefficient spending and reinvest in campaigns with stronger historical performance.

## 💰 Budget Reallocation Strategy

The proposed strategy reallocates existing expenditure rather than increasing the total marketing budget.

| Action | Proposed Amount |
|---|---:|
| Reduce spending on weak campaigns | ₹660.2K |
| Reinvest in proven winners | ₹660.2K |
| Net budget change | ₹0 |

The strategy prioritizes campaigns with ROAS below 1.5x for review, reduction, pausing, or creative improvement. Reallocated funds should support high-performing campaigns with meaningful purchase volume.

## 📈 The 25% Sales Growth Target

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Current Shopify sales | ₹14.30M |
| Target Shopify sales | ₹17.87M |
| Required incremental sales | ₹3.57M |
| Current paid-media spend | ₹6.28M |
| Current sales-to-spend ratio | 2.28x |
| Required ratio at unchanged spend | 2.85x |
| Required efficiency improvement | 25% |

The historical-ROAS scenario for the proposed reallocation estimates approximately ₹3.64M in incremental revenue, slightly exceeding the ₹3.57M sales gap.

**Important:** This is a scenario based on historical performance, not a guaranteed outcome. Actual results may differ because of diminishing returns, changes in conversion rates, and campaign scaling limitations.

## 🔍 Analysis Approach

The project follows a business-focused analytical framework:

1. **Performance analysis:** Compare marketing channels using expenditure and ROAS.
2. **Campaign diagnosis:** Identify high-performing campaigns, weak campaigns, and inefficient creatives.
3. **Budget optimization:** Determine where expenditure can be reduced and where it can be reinvested.
4. **Growth scenario analysis:** Evaluate the efficiency required to achieve the sales target at a fixed budget.
5. **Validation strategy:** Recommend controlled experiments to test whether the proposed changes deliver sustainable improvements.

## 🚀 Recommended Actions

1. Reduce or pause persistently underperforming campaigns with ROAS below 1.5x.
2. Scale the strongest Google campaigns gradually rather than increasing their budgets abruptly.
3. Protect and expand high-volume Facebook campaigns, particularly MOF & BOF.
4. Replicate successful creative concepts and rework weak Flat 40% and Special Prices advertisements.
5. Run structured seven-day scaling, creative, and landing-page tests with explicit ROAS and conversion guardrails.

## 🧪 Validation & Measurement

The recommendations should be validated through controlled experiments.

Key evaluation criteria include:

- ROAS before and after budget reallocation.
- Purchase volume and conversion performance.
- Revenue generated by individual campaigns.
- Performance changes as campaign budgets increase.
- Creative and landing-page test outcomes.

Budget increases should be gradual, with predefined guardrails to identify declining efficiency before additional spending amplifies waste.

## 💡 Business Impact

The analysis identifies a potential path to improve marketing efficiency and support sales growth without increasing total paid-media expenditure.

The central insight is that **growth may come from allocating the existing budget more effectively, rather than simply spending more**.

The proposed approach combines campaign-level diagnosis, disciplined budget reallocation, creative optimization, and experimental validation to turn marketing data into actionable business decisions.
